How to use these guides

Begin with the on-page checklist, then check page titles and internal links. Review the SEO audit materials when you want to repeat the process across a whole website.

  1. Give each important page one clear topic and useful answer.
  2. Write descriptive titles and internal link text rather than forcing repeated keywords.
  3. Check images, content gaps and crawlable navigation before adding more pages.
QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Do I need an exact keyword in every heading?

No. Use natural headings that explain the section. Include important terms where they help readers understand it.

Should every page link to all other pages?

No. Link to useful, related pages that help someone complete the task.

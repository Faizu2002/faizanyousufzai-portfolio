How to use these guides

Start with the local search overview. Then review your Google Business Profile, location and service pages, local citations and tracking. Use the ranking and analysis guides to investigate a drop or a competitor that appears above you.

  1. Keep business name, services, hours and contact details accurate on your profile and website.
  2. Create useful location and service pages with real details, not repeated city names.
  3. Review Search Console, Maps performance, reviews and citations together.
QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Where should I start with local SEO?

Check Google Business Profile accuracy, your main service pages and mobile usability before moving to advanced link and citation work.

Are Maps results and regular website rankings the same?

No. They overlap, but local results also depend on relevance, distance and prominence.

Keyword Research Guides: Search Intent & Clustering

Good keyword research is not a list of terms to copy into a page. It connects what people search for with the kind of page they want to find. These guides show how to group related queries, look for gaps in competing sites and decide which keywords deserve their own pages.

How to use these guides

Start with competitor keyword research and keyword clustering. Then use the long-tail and next-step guides to turn a spreadsheet into a practical page plan.

  1. Group keywords around a shared search intent, not only matching words.
  2. Inspect what kind of page users expect: a tool, guide, service or product.
  3. Prioritize topics you can answer clearly and support with internal links.
QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

What is keyword clustering?

It is grouping searches that one useful page can answer without splitting the same intent across many URLs.

Do low-volume keywords matter?

Yes. Specific queries can be useful when they match your expertise and lead to a clear next step.

Ecommerce SEO Guides: Product & Category Pages

Ecommerce SEO needs pages that help buyers compare and choose products. Category pages organize inventory and target broader questions, while product pages answer detailed buying questions. Technical setup, filters, internal links and accurate product information all affect discovery.

How to use these guides

Start with the ecommerce checklist. Then move to category and product page SEO, and use ecommerce keyword research when mapping the right searches to page types.

  1. Make important products and categories reachable with normal HTML links.
  2. Use detailed product descriptions and helpful category copy.
  3. Control filter and faceted URLs to avoid crawl waste or duplicate pages.
QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Should product and category pages target the same keywords?

Not usually. Match broader browsing intent to categories and specific product intent to product pages.

Should every filter URL be indexed?

No. Index only useful, distinct pages that answer real searches.

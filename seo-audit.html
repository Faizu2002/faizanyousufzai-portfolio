SEO Audit

SEO audits that turn problems into a clear fix order.

An SEO audit should explain what is wrong, why it matters, which URLs are affected and what should be fixed first. I combine technical checks, search data, page quality and implementation context instead of exporting a long tool report.

Audit the pages that matter first

A useful audit starts with the business goal and the pages that should attract or convert search traffic. This stops low-impact warnings from taking over the report.

I group findings by impact, confidence and implementation effort so the site owner can see what deserves attention first.

Combine tools with manual review

Crawlers and audit tools are useful for scale, but they do not understand every page goal. Manual review is needed for search intent, content overlap, internal linking and whether a recommendation makes sense for the site.

Search Console data can also show which queries and pages are already earning impressions, which helps protect working URLs while fixing weak areas.

Finish with validation, not a PDF

The audit is only useful when changes are implemented and checked. Important fixes should be validated after deployment so redirects, canonicals, links, schema and indexing signals work as intended.

A short prioritized roadmap is often more valuable than a report with hundreds of unranked warnings.

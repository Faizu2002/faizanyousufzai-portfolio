Privacy policy

This policy explains the basic information this website may process when you browse the site or contact me.

Information you provide

If you email, use a contact method, submit a form, or otherwise contact me, I may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, website URL and message, so I can respond to your request.

Analytics and technical information

Analytics or hosting services may process standard technical and usage information such as pages viewed, browser or device type, approximate location, referrer, IP address, performance data and timestamps. This information may be used to understand site usage, maintain security and improve the website.

Cookies and similar technologies

The core website can be browsed without creating an account. Some site features, analytics, authentication services or advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Where consent is required by applicable law, advertising or non-essential tracking should be handled in accordance with those requirements.

External links

This website links to third-party websites, official sources and social platforms. Those websites are responsible for their own privacy, security and data practices.

Tools and calculations

Most public calculators on this website run in the browser and are designed to provide informational estimates. Do not enter sensitive personal information unless a tool specifically requires it and clearly explains how that information is handled.

Data retention

Messages or project enquiries may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records or meet legal and security requirements. Third-party services may apply their own retention periods.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional information, adjust cookie settings in your browser, or contact me about privacy questions related to information you have directly provided through this website.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics, advertising partners or other services change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.

How I document results

No invented rankings. No mystery numbers.

If a result can be inspected publicly, I show it. That can include category architecture, live tools, structured pages, navigation, responsive UI, schema or clean URLs.

Traffic, rankings and conversions need reliable measurement data. Where that data is not available, I do not manufacture a percentage just to make the case study look stronger.

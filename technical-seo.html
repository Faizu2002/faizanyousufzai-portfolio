What this service covers

I review the technical signals that can stop search engines from accessing, understanding or selecting the right URL. The work is focused on practical fixes, not an oversized checklist.

  • Crawl paths and status codes
  • Indexing and canonical signals
  • XML sitemaps and robots.txt
  • Schema markup and structured data
  • Core Web Vitals and performance
  • Internal link discoverability

How I approach the work

I start with the real problem and the pages that matter. That keeps the audit useful and stops low-impact warnings from taking over the project. Findings are grouped by impact and implementation effort, then validated after changes are made.

What you receive

Clear findings, examples from the site, recommended fixes and implementation notes. When the project fits HTML, CSS, JavaScript or WordPress, I can also handle the front-end changes directly.

What I do not promise

I do not guarantee a fixed Google position. Rankings depend on many systems outside any consultant’s control. The focus is on better technical quality, relevance, discoverability and useful search traffic.

Calendar-based result

Years, months and days.

The calculator counts completed years first, then completed months, then leftover days. This handles different month lengths and leap years better than simply dividing the number of days.

How to calculate exact age online

Enter the date of birth and the date you want to calculate age on. The result shows completed years, remaining months and remaining days, plus the total number of days between the selected dates.

Why age can differ from a simple day count

Months do not all have the same number of days, and leap years add an extra day to February. A calendar age calculation follows the real calendar instead of assuming every year or month has a fixed length.

Common uses

You can use the age calculator for school forms, job applications, eligibility checks, birthdays or any situation where an exact age on a specific date is needed.

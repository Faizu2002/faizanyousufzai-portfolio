FAST 2026 criteria

Program-specific selection weights.

For 2026, FAST-NUCES publishes 50% admission test, 40% HSSC and 10% SSC for computing and listed business programs. For engineering, the published weights are 33% test, 50% HSSC and 17% SSC.

FAST also states that its own admission test has negative marking. Enter the final adjusted test percentage you are using for your merit estimate.

FAST aggregate calculator 2026

This FAST-NUCES aggregate calculator applies the university's published undergraduate selection weights for computing, business and engineering program groups. Choose your program group first because engineering uses a different formula.

FAST aggregate formula for computing and business

For the listed computing programs and BBA, Accounting & Finance, Business Analytics and Financial Technology, the current published selection criteria use 50% admission test, 40% HSSC/equivalent and 10% SSC/equivalent.

FAST aggregate formula for engineering

For the listed engineering programs, the current selection criteria use 33% admission test, 50% HSSC/equivalent and 17% SSC/equivalent.

Negative marking and final merit

FAST states that there is negative marking in the NU admission test. This calculator does not guess the penalty from raw correct and wrong answers because test instructions can change. Enter your adjusted test percentage. Final cut-off marks are determined by FAST and can differ by program and admission cycle.

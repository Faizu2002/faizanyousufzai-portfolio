Salary tax calculator Pakistan for 2026-27 and 2025-26

This salary tax calculator Pakistan tool gives a simple estimate of tax on salary income. Enter a monthly or annual salary, choose a tax year, and the calculator shows annual income tax, monthly tax, monthly salary after tax and the effective tax rate.

The page is also useful as an income tax calculator Pakistan for people whose main taxable income is salary. It is not meant for business income, property income or freelancer export withholding because those can follow different rules.

How to use the tax calculator

Choose the tax year first. Then select monthly or annual salary and enter your gross taxable salary. Press Calculate Salary Tax. If you enter a monthly amount, the tool converts it to an annual figure before applying the selected slab.

What the result shows

The result gives five useful numbers: annual salary, annual income tax, estimated monthly tax, monthly salary after tax and effective tax rate. The effective rate is usually lower than the highest slab rate because Pakistan uses progressive salary tax slabs.

Tax calculator 2025-26 for Pakistan

If you searched for a tax calculator 2025-26, choose 2025-26 · Tax Year 2026 in the calculator. The tool then uses the salary rules saved for that year instead of the current 2026-27 rules.

This is useful when checking an older payslip, comparing tax between two budgets, reviewing a job offer from that year or understanding how much salary tax applied before the latest budget changes.

Salary tax calculator 2025-26

The salary tax calculator 2025-26 option starts with a 0% slab up to Rs. 600,000 annual taxable salary. Above that level, tax rises by slab. For this year, the calculator also includes the salary surcharge rule stored for income above Rs. 10 million.

Salary tax slab 2025-26

The salary tax slab 2025-26 table appears automatically when you select 2025-26. You can use it to see the base tax and marginal rate for each income range. This makes the page useful even if you only want to check the slab and do not need a calculation.

Tax slab 2025-26 explained in simple words

A tax slab does not mean your full salary is taxed at one rate. Only the part of income that falls inside a higher band is charged at that band’s rate. That is why two people with different salaries can have different effective tax rates even when part of their income reaches the same slab.

Income tax calculator Pakistan for salaried people

This income tax calculator Pakistan page is built for salaried people. For a quick estimate, use your normal gross taxable salary before salary tax is deducted. If your employer gives taxable allowances or benefits, your real taxable salary may be different from your basic salary.

Monthly salary vs annual salary

If your salary is Rs. 150,000 per month, the calculator treats it as Rs. 1,800,000 per year. If you already know your annual taxable salary, select the annual option so the tool does not multiply it by 12 again.

Monthly tax deduction

The monthly tax shown by this tool is the estimated annual tax divided by 12. Your actual employer deduction can be different because payroll systems may adjust tax during the year for bonuses, arrears, tax credits, exemptions or earlier deductions.

Pakistan salary tax slabs 2026-27

For 2026-27, which corresponds to Tax Year 2027, the current FBR material describes reduced rates for salaried taxpayers through restructured slabs and a higher threshold for the maximum 35% rate. The calculator keeps 2026-27 as the default year because it is the current period on this page.

2026-27 vs 2025-26

The year selector lets you compare the same salary under both years. Enter a salary, calculate it for 2026-27, then switch to 2025-26 and calculate again. This is an easy way to see how a budget change can affect estimated salary tax.

What is salary after tax?

Salary after tax is the amount left after estimated salary income tax is removed. This calculator shows a simple monthly take-home figure after income tax only. It does not subtract provident fund, EOBI, insurance, loan deductions or other company deductions.

Why your actual FBR tax can be different

The calculator is designed for quick planning. Final tax can change because of taxable benefits, bonuses, other income, exemptions, tax credits, adjustments, employer payroll methods or changes in law. FBR also notes that its rate cards are a ready reference and the law itself prevails if there is a conflict.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best tax calculator for salary in Pakistan?

A useful calculator should let you choose the correct tax year, show the slab used, explain the result and separate annual tax from monthly deduction. This page does all four for salary income.

Can I use this tax calculator for 2025-26?

Yes. Select 2025-26 · Tax Year 2026. The calculator will switch the slab table and calculation to that year.

Is this a salary tax calculator Pakistan tool?

Yes. It is made specifically for Pakistan salary income and includes multiple FBR tax years.

Can it calculate income tax in Pakistan?

It calculates estimated income tax on salary. It does not calculate every type of income tax, such as business, property, capital gains or export withholding.

Where can I see tax slab 2025-26?

Select 2025-26 in the calculator. The slab table under the results will update to show the tax slab 2025-26 used by the tool.

What is the difference between marginal tax rate and effective tax rate?

The marginal rate is the rate on the top part of your taxable salary. The effective rate is your total estimated tax divided by total annual salary.

Does this calculator file my FBR return?

No. It only gives an estimate. It does not submit a tax return, create a payment slip or connect to your FBR account.

What is the official source for the tax rates?

The tool uses FBR rate-card and Finance Act information as its reference. You can check the FBR Withholding Tax Rate Cards for current and previous years.

Salary tax calculator Pakistan for 2026-27 and 2025-26

This Pakistan salary tax calculator turns a monthly or annual salary into an estimated annual tax, monthly tax and monthly salary after tax. The default setting uses the current 2026-27 salary tax slabs, while the year selector lets you calculate older periods including 2025-26, 2024-25, 2023-24 and 2022-23.

How the Pakistan income tax calculator works

If you enter a monthly salary, the tool multiplies it by 12 to estimate annual taxable salary. It then applies the selected FBR salary tax slab and divides the annual tax by 12 for a simple monthly estimate. The effective tax rate is annual tax divided by annual salary.

Salary tax slab 2025-26

For 2025-26, which is Tax Year 2026, salaried tax starts above annual taxable income of Rs. 600,000. The selected-year table above shows each bracket and its formula. For taxable income above Rs. 10 million in that year, the calculator also includes the 9% salary surcharge on calculated income tax provided by the Finance Act 2025 rules.

Salary tax slab 2026-27

For 2026-27, or Tax Year 2027, the first Rs. 600,000 remains in the 0% slab. The schedule then moves through 1%, 11%, 20%, 25%, 29%, 32% and 35% marginal rates as taxable salary rises. The maximum 35% slab starts above Rs. 7 million under the 2026 schedule.

What is included in monthly take-home salary?

The take-home figure shown here is salary minus this calculator's estimated salary income tax only. It does not subtract provident fund, EOBI, loan deductions, insurance, employer benefits or any other payroll item.

Why your actual salary tax can be different

Real payroll tax can differ because the Income Tax Ordinance includes rules for exemptions, tax credits, deductions, other income, adjustments and employer withholding. Treat the result as a planning estimate and confirm a final filing or payroll position with FBR guidance or a qualified tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

What is the tax-free salary limit in Pakistan for 2026-27?
The first Rs. 600,000 of annual taxable salary is in the 0% salary slab for Tax Year 2027.

Can I use this as a salary tax calculator 2025-26?
Yes. Select 2025-26 / Tax Year 2026 from the tax-year dropdown before calculating.

Does it work as an income tax calculator Pakistan tool?
It is designed specifically for salary income. Business income, freelancer export withholding, property income and other tax categories use different rules.

Where can I check the official tax card?
Use the FBR Withholding Tax Rate Cards page for the current and previous official rate cards.

About Faizan

I learn SEO by building, testing and fixing real websites.

My work sits between search strategy and web development. That means I can understand the problem in Search Console or a crawl, then get into the page structure and code when the fix belongs there.

My story

From SEO basics to building search-friendly products.

I started learning SEO in January 2025. By July 2025, I had moved into professional backlinking work and kept expanding into technical SEO, on-page optimization, content structure and front-end development.

Instead of treating development and SEO as separate jobs, I like using both together. A site can look polished and still be hard to crawl. It can also be technically perfect and fail to answer the search intent. My goal is to connect both sides.

My public projects include ToolsForAll, a browser-based utility platform, HANAB, a perfume e-commerce website for Pakistan, and this portfolio, which also acts as my SEO knowledge base.
